# System Architecture
The Simplif-i Operational Command Suite consists of nine integrated modules, functioning as a single sovereign operating layer:

1. **Command Dashboard**: Unified Operational Intelligence. Live operational signal pulling from every module in real time.
2. **GRC Engine**: Governance, Risk & Compliance. Mapping principal risks to specific controls, owners, and live evidence.
3. **PMO**: Portfolio Velocity & Delivery Governance. Connecting delivery to governance with value-centric measurement.
4. **Contracts**: Full Lifecycle Management. Clause-level mapping to compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, GDPR, SOC 2).
5. **Company Secretary**: Statutory Compliance & Corporate Governance. Ensuring readiness for ECCTA 2023 and Provision 29.
6. **M&A Pro+**: Deal Lifecycle & Post-Merger Integration. Linking deal room findings to operational action items.
7. **Training & Competency**: Automated Evidence. Closing the loop between training delivery and GRC evidence.
8. **Procurement & RFP**: Automated Vendor Due Diligence. Profiling, scoring, and monitoring supply chain risk.
9. **AI Governance**: Model Risk & Exposure. Governing AI usage with the same rigour as financial controls.
